● Here's a summary of how prices are extracted from each retailer:

  ---
  Billa (billa_scraper.py)

  - Source: ssbbilla.site — an accessibility (screen-reader) version of the BILLA weekly brochure with full structured text (not images/flipbook)
  - Method: Scraped via Firecrawl MCP as markdown, then parsed with regex patterns
  - Price extraction: Regex against the markdown text to find BGN prices (лв.) and distinguish promo vs. regular prices

  ---
  Gladen.bg / Hit Max (gladen_html_scraper.py)

  - Source: r — paginated HTML promotions listing
  - Method: Direct HTTP requests (requests library) across up to 42 pages (≈1,000 products / 24 per page)
  - Price extraction: HTML regex targeting specific CSS classes:
    - product-card-price-current is-promo → promo price
    - product-card-price-old → regular price
    - Only keeps items where promo < regular (genuine discounts)

  There's also an older markdown-based parser (gladen_scraper.py) that parsed Firecrawl markdown output using BGN regex (\d+\.\d{2})\s*лв\. — replaced by the HTML
   scraper.

  ---
  Fantastico (fantastico_pdf_parser.py)

  - Source: Fantastico's brochure PDF (downloaded from their FlippingBook viewer)
  - Method: pdfplumber — extracts words with bounding boxes (x/y coordinates), then groups them into spatial columns
  - Price extraction: Finds BGN price words matching \d{1,3}[.,]\d{2}\s*ЛВ\., uses column geometry to associate nearby text as the product name, and infers       
  regular price from EUR prices (converted at 1.95583 rate) or discount % (-19% etc.)

  - There's also an older OCR pipeline (fantastico_ocr_pipeline.py) using Azure Document Intelligence (prebuilt-read model) for when the PDF text is not
  machine-readable (scanned images).

  ---
  Common pattern across all

  All parsers normalize to the same schema: product_name, promo_price, regular_price, unit, promo_period, source_store, extraction_date — and merge into a single 
  bulgarian_promo_prices_merged.json master file with deduplication.
